Small comforts, real life

A little support.

For the days when you need to be reminded you're human.

One minute to yourself

Unclench your jaw. Let your shoulders lower. Notice the ground beneath your feet. You do not need to fix everything in this minute.

Three things you can tell yourself

“I am allowed to be tired.”

“A hard day does not make me a bad mom.”

“I can ask for help.”

A caring note: These reflections are for emotional support, not a replacement for professional mental health care. If you are in immediate danger or a crisis in the US, call 911 or call/text 988 for support.

LITTLE THINGS THAT HELP

The gentle resource library.

Not another to-do list. Just a few small places to rest.

you're welcome here ♡
Take what you need

A moment of care, right now.

You're allowed to start small. Even thirty seconds of softness counts.

01pause for a breath

The one-minute reset

Place both feet on the ground. Unclench your jaw. Let your shoulders drop. Breathe at a pace that feels comfortable and name one thing you can see.

You don't have to solve everything in this minute.

02let it be enough

Three reminders for hard days

“I can love my family and need quiet.”

“Rest doesn't have to be earned.”

“A difficult day doesn't define me.”

03reach out gently

A message you can send

“Hey, I'm having a heavy day. I don't need you to fix it, but I'd really appreciate someone listening. Do you have a moment?”

Asking for connection is a form of strength.

These resources are for encouragement and general wellness, not professional medical or mental health advice. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988. If there is immediate danger, call 911.
